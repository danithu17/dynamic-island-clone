## 7. Telemetry & Crash Data Policy
- **Zero Telemetry**: Aura Island contains no analytics tracking, no user profiling, and no advertising modules.
- **Local Logs**: Crash and diagnostic logs (`aura-island.log`) are stored strictly locally in `%APPDATA%\AuraIsland\` on your computer and are never uploaded to any remote service.
